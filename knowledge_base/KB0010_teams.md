# Microsoft Teams Troubleshooting

## Teams is frozen or not responding
1. Quit Teams completely: right-click the Teams icon in the system tray and choose Quit.
2. Start Teams again. The AI helpdesk agent can restart the Teams application on your laptop remotely.
3. If it still freezes, clear the Teams cache: quit Teams, delete the contents of `%appdata%\Microsoft\Teams`, and start it again.

## Audio and video problems in meetings
- Choose the correct device under Settings > Devices and make a test call.
- Close other applications that use the camera or microphone.
- Wired headsets are more reliable than Bluetooth in large meetings.

## Messages not updating
Sign out and in again, and check your network or VPN connection.

## When to escalate
Raise an incident for Collaboration Support if Teams crashes after the cache is cleared or a meeting room device does not work.
